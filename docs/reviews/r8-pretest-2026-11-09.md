## 3. Possibilities found

1. **The analysis is reusable for the forward log:** one draw path
   (`draw.mjs` + `nulls.mjs`) serves capture and analysis and re-draws the
   capture's trades exactly (0 mismatches on 1,946 groups).
2. **1 m klines give exact trade counts per minute**, so a uniform draw over
   a busy hour needs three minute reads, not the whole tape.
3. **The 1 s mark history exists for the whole period**, every second present
   in every window read — a free, exact price record of the past.
4. **The fills route reads an account back to its first fill**, so R2's page
   cap can be lifted where it matters (132 reads for three accounts).
5. **A day-level clustering catches what trader-level clustering misses:**
   entries of many traders on the same day share the market.
